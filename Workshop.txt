description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=Tired of pulling grass by hand just to get a nice lawn?
description=And you don’t want to turn your garden into an ugly patch of dirt?
description=Thanks to this mod, you can finally mow your lawn properly with a scythe, bring back its former beauty, and even sow new grass!
description=
description=[h2]How does it work?[/h2][list]
description=[*] A new option, "Mow the lawn", appears in the Gardening context sub menu when you have a scythe in your inventory. You can also scythe dead crops along Knox County. The scythe’s regular behavior remains unchanged: you can still create farmland but the option is named now "Uproot grass" (tell me if you have a better idea).
description=[*] Use the rotate key to change the cursor size: 3x3, 2x2, or 1x1 (just like with regular scythe use, in case you didn’t know).
description=[*] The time required depends on the number of grass tufts to cut.
description=[*] Also works with the hand scythe, but the cursor size is limited to 1x1 (still way faster than doing it all by hand).
description=[*] Clearing grass has a chance to yield [b]Grass Seeds[/b].
description=[*] You can sow [b]5 Grass Seeds[/b] on dirt (include player placed dirt using a dirt bag) to let fresh grass grow over time. Grass growth speed is tied to the vanilla sandbox option: [i]Livestock -> Grass Regrowth Time[/i] (default: 240 hours).
description=[*] A new option, "Rake Leaves", lets you collect fallen leaves with a rake.
description=[*] Fallen leaves can be used to light a fire (or feed one, though they won’t burn for long) or added to a compost bin.
description=[*] You can now cut down certain bushes that cannot be cut down in vanilla.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Limitations[/h2][list]
description=[*] [b]Grass Regrowth:[/b] Sowing grass seeds relies on the game's native grass regrowth system. Due to how vanilla engine handles ground updates, you need to leave the area (unload the chunk) and come back later to see the visual change once the grass has regrown.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Planned features[/h2][list]
